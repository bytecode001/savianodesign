Oliva is a fictional restaurant created to showcase a web design project. The name, spaces, menu, prices, hours and services are demonstration content. They do not represent a business open to the public. The photographs are AI-generated.

Forms and personal data

The forms do not send data, emails or booking requests. Entered information is used only to show a confirmation within the page. The site code does not save it in cookies, localStorage or a database.

Language preference

The site may save your chosen language in browser localStorage. Remove this preference by clearing site data in your browser settings. We do not use analytics or advertising tools.

External services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts: your browser makes requests to Google servers. Links to Google Maps and SavianoDesign open only when selected. The hosting provider may process technical connection data under its own privacy policy.
